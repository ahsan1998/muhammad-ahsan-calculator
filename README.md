# Renewal Runway — Recertification Pace Calculator

A browser-based tool for professionals tracking continuing education credits toward credential renewal. Enter how many credits you need, how many you have, and your cycle end date — it tells you the weekly pace required to finish on time.

**Why this over a generic calculator?** KnowledgeCity's learners renew SHRM, HRCI, and PMI credentials on three-year cycles. Many leave it to the last few months and need a quick answer to "am I on pace?" — not a spreadsheet. Renewal Runway handles the edge cases (less than a week left, requirement already met, deadline passed) and flags an at-risk pace before it becomes a crisis.

## Assumptions

- **Totals only** — the calculator tracks overall credit totals. Category sub-requirements (e.g. SHRM's PDC mix or PMI's Talent Triangle splits) are out of scope.
- **1 credit ≈ 1 contact hour** — the at-risk threshold of 40 credits/week is treated as equivalent to full-time study. Pace above that is flagged as at-risk.
- **Both dates are inclusive** — a cycle running Dec 29 to Dec 31 has 3 days remaining, not 2.
- **Pace rounds up** — the displayed weekly pace always guarantees completion by the deadline; it never rounds down.
- **"Calculate from" defaults to today** — re-read at page load and again when Reset is pressed, so the default is always the current date.
- **Preset credit totals** — SHRM-CP/SCP 60, HRCI PHR/SPHR 60, PMI PMP 60, PMI CAPM 15. These reflect published renewal requirements as of November 2026.

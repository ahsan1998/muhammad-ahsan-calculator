# Renewal Runway — Recertification Pace Calculator

**[Live demo](https://muhammad-ahsan-calculator.vercel.app/)**

A browser-based tool for professionals tracking continuing education credits toward credential renewal. Enter how many credits you need, how many you have, and your cycle end date — it tells you the weekly pace required to finish on time. L&D managers can switch to a team view to enter up to 20 members at once and see them ranked by risk.

**Why this over a generic calculator?** KnowledgeCity's learners renew SHRM, HRCI, and PMI credentials on three-year cycles. Many leave it to the last few months and need a quick answer to "am I on pace?" — not a spreadsheet. Renewal Runway handles the edge cases (less than a week left, requirement already met, deadline passed) and flags an at-risk pace before it becomes a crisis.

---

## Try it in 30 seconds

**My renewal tab** — select SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP, type 20 in credits earned, set Calculate from to Jan 1 2027, Cycle end date to Mar 11 2027. Result: **4 credits per week · 40 credits remaining · 70 days left**.

**Team view tab** — set Calculate from to Jan 1 2027, then add two members: Ali (PMI PMP, earned 10, end Dec 15 2026) and Sara (SHRM-CP / SHRM-SCP, earned 45, end Dec 31 2027). Summary: **1 of 2 at risk or lapsed**. Ali ranks first with a **Lapsed** badge and "Cycle ended 17 days ago."; Sara ranks second with an **On track** badge and "0.29 credits/week".

## Running the tests

```
node --test src/calc.test.js
```

74 tests: calculation and validation logic for S-1 through S-8 and S-10 (ranking, tie-breaks, badge classification, summary counts, empty-name fallback), plus a script-syntax check that parses every inline `<script>` block in `index.html` via `new Function()`. **S-9 (reset button)** and the team-view DOM interactions depend on the page itself, so they are not in the unit-test suite. They, and every other implemented story, were checked in a real browser (Chrome) by a scripted walkthrough of the acceptance criteria against both `src/index.html` and the live demo, including a 375px phone-width check.

## Assumptions

- **Totals only** — the calculator tracks overall credit totals. Category sub-requirements (e.g. SHRM's PDC mix or PMI's Talent Triangle splits) are out of scope.
- **1 credit ≈ 1 contact hour** — the at-risk threshold of 40 credits/week is treated as equivalent to full-time study. Pace above that is flagged as at-risk.
- **Both dates are inclusive** — a cycle running Dec 29 to Dec 31 has 3 days remaining, not 2.
- **Pace rounds up** — the displayed weekly pace always guarantees completion by the deadline; it never rounds down.
- **"Calculate from" defaults to today** — re-read at page load and again when Reset is pressed, so the default is always the current date.
- **Preset credit totals** — SHRM-CP/SCP 60, HRCI PHR/SPHR 60, PMI PMP 60, PMI CAPM 15. These reflect published renewal requirements as of November 2026.

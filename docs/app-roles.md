# Renewal Runway — App Roles

## R-1 · Certified Professional

A Certified Professional is a credentialed HR or project-management practitioner who is responsible for renewing their own certification before their cycle ends. They know their total credit requirement and their cycle end date, and they check in on their own progress when they feel uncertain about whether they are keeping up. They can enter their certification details — credits required, credits already earned, and cycle end date — and see their required weekly pace from today and a risk assessment for their renewal. They must never be told they are on track, or that their renewal is complete, when their actual credit figures say otherwise.

## R-2 · L&D Manager

An L&D Manager is a learning-and-development or team manager who monitors recertification progress for people who report to them. They are checking someone else's renewal: they read credit figures from that person's certification portal and work in quarterly planning windows rather than reacting to today's status. They can enter a team member's certification details and choose a future "calculate from" date to model what pace the team member would need if training time started next quarter or later. Their primary output is the risk flag — they use it to decide where to direct training budget and protected learning time. They must never receive a result that shows a renewal as low-risk or on track when the underlying figures indicate the team member is at risk of missing their deadline.
